### Deployment Prometheus and Node exporter


Prometheus collects and stores its metrics as **time series data**, i.e. metrics information is stored with the timestamp at which it was recorded, alongside optional key-value pairs called labels. [Prometheus Docker Image](https://hub.docker.com/r/prom/prometheus)
 
The Prometheus Node Exporter exposes a wide variety of hardware- and kernel-related metrics. [Node Exporter Docker Image](https://hub.docker.com/r/prom/node-exporter)
